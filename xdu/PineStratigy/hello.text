\subsection{1. Gaussian Channel}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Details}: Utilizes a Gaussian channel with a mean (20-period SMA) and an upper band (mean + 2 standard deviations). Entry occurs when price exceeds the upper band and Stochastic RSI \%K > \%D.
    \item \textbf{Mathematical Concept}:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Mean}: \( \text{Mean} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{20} \text{price}_i}{20} \), where \(\text{price}\) is the closing price over 20 periods, representing the average level.
        \item \textbf{Standard Deviation (\(\sigma\))}: \( \sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{20} (\text{price}_i - \text{mean})^2}{20}} \), measuring price volatility around the mean.
        \item \textbf{Upper Band}: \( \text{mean} + 2 \times \sigma \), assuming a normal distribution where \(\sim\)95\% of data lies within 2\(\sigma\).
        \item \textbf{Condition}: \( \text{price} > \text{upper\_band} \land \text{stochrsi\_k} > \text{stochrsi\_d} \), where \%K > \%D confirms bullish momentum.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Logic}: Assumes prices deviating 2\(\sigma\) above the mean indicate a strong breakout, validated by upward momentum.
    \item \textbf{Why Good Returns}: Excels in volatile, trending markets by capturing statistically significant breakouts with sustained momentum.
\end{itemize}
